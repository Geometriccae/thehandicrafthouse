Complete Lovable AI Prompt

Design a premium handcrafted marketplace inspired by Scandinavian minimalism, organic interiors, botanical aesthetics, and luxury editorial magazines. The website should feel calm, warm, timeless, and elegant, using generous white space, earthy neutral colors, soft rounded corners, subtle paper and linen textures, organic section dividers, editorial typography, and natural photography. Use a palette of warm ivory, sand, sage green, terracotta, olive, walnut brown, and muted gold accents. Include slow, refined micro-animations such as fade-ins, gentle hover lifts, image zooms, and floating botanical elements. Build a fully responsive, SEO-friendly website with reusable React components, modern accessibility practices, and a polished design system. The experience should resemble a high-end artisan boutique rather than a typical online store, emphasizing storytelling, craftsmanship, sustainability, and authenticity while maintaining a clean, luxurious, and nature-inspired interface.
